## Part A：一句话问题定义

> How can we detect whether the physical preconditions implicitly assumed by an LLM planner—such as sufficient ground friction, manageable incline, and adequate load capacity—hold true in the real environment, *before* the robot executes the plan, without relying on the LLM itself to perform the verification?

（54 words）

---

## Part B：问题展开

### 段 1：具身AI 的新风险——LLM 规划器引入了传统机器人没有的攻击面

Embodied AI systems increasingly rely on large language models (LLMs) as task planners, translating natural language instructions into physical action sequences. This architectural shift introduces a qualitatively new attack surface absent from traditional robotics. BADROBOT (Zhang et al., ICLR 2025) first demonstrated that LLM planners can be jailbroken through natural language prompts to output physically dangerous instructions, identifying three distinct attack paths—contextual jailbreak, safety misalignment, and conceptual deception—all exploiting the fact that LLM safety alignment is trained in text space but executed in physical space. Blindfold (Huang et al., SenSys 2026) automated this attack by decomposing malicious intents into individually benign atomic actions (e.g., "explode the user's phone" → `find(phone) → pick(phone) → move(oven) → stretch()`), achieving 93.2% attack success rate on GPT-4o and bypassing all existing defenses including Llama-Guard, SafeDecoding, and VeriSafe. Causal-Plan-Bench (Lu et al., 2026, Tsinghua/MSRA) further demonstrated that this is not merely an adversarial vulnerability but a systematic capability deficit: current vision-language models are "superficial token predictors" rather than physically grounded causal reasoners, scoring only 38.18 (Gemini 3 Pro) and 45.28 (best trained model) on a 1,200-instance diagnostic benchmark. The core issue is the **semantic-physical gap**: an instruction that appears semantically benign can produce catastrophic physical consequences when executed in a specific physical context.

### 段 2：社区已经识别了这个问题——Grounding Failure 有多篇独立文献记录

This problem—LLM planners producing plans that are logically coherent but physically infeasible—has been independently documented across multiple research threads under the term **grounding failure**. Chakraborty et al. (2024) provided the first quantitative evidence, showing that LLM agent hallucination rates increase by 40% under scene-task inconsistencies. Han et al. (2024) documented the now-classic case: an LLM reasoning that during a fire, the robot should navigate to the server room because "server rooms have fire suppression systems = safe," ignoring that server rooms are not emergency exits. A systematic study at EMNLP 2025 Findings tested 12 models and found that hallucination rates can be up to 40× higher under scene-task inconsistencies than under consistent prompts. Most recently, Causal-Plan-Bench (Lu et al., 2026) established a 1,200-instance diagnostic suite across four causal dimensions (executability, effects, composition, robustness) and demonstrated that even the strongest models systematically fail at physically grounded reasoning. Critically, grounding failure is not a random error—it is a **systematic consequence** of the fact that LLMs optimize for linguistic coherence rather than physical causality. The LLM does not "know" that it does not know the coefficient of friction of the floor it is about to traverse.

### 段 3：现有防御为什么不够——三条路线共享一个根本盲区

Existing defenses against grounding failure and semantic-physical gap attacks fall into three lines of work, each with a structural limitation:

**Line 1: Improve the LLM planner.** ConceptAgent (Rivera et al., 2024) has the LLM generate PDDL-style preconditions for each action and checks them against a 3D scene graph before execution. However, the verification specification is generated by the same LLM that does the planning—an instance of "LLM reviewing LLM." The precondition space is limited to 17 Boolean predicates, making continuous physical quantities (friction, incline, load) inexpressible. DDCG (Ma et al., NeurIPS 2025 Workshop LAW) decouples feasibility evaluation (C_F, a RoBERTa classifier) from quality evaluation (C_Q). But C_F is trained on VirtualHome text data with human-defined violation rules—it learns to recognize only the patterns its designers anticipated, and it takes only text descriptions as input (max 256 tokens), with no access to sensor data. Causal Reasoner (Lu et al., 2026) trains VLMs on causal reasoning traces, improving benchmark scores from 33.22 to 45.28—but this is still far from reliable, and the approach aims to make LLMs better rather than to verify their outputs independently. **All three have been bypassed by Blindfold.**

**Line 2: Formal verification of LLM outputs.** VerifyLLM (Grigorev et al., IROS 2025) converts natural language plans to Linear Temporal Logic (LTL) and uses an LLM to verify logical coherence. However, LTL expresses discrete temporal properties, not continuous physical constraints. The formula "friction must exceed μ ≥ tan(θ) × (m_load / m_robot)" has no natural LTL representation. SAFER (Khan et al., IROS 2025) employs a multi-LLM architecture with a dedicated Safety LLM auditing plans against 15 risk criteria—but the Safety LLM is itself an LLM, sharing the same fundamental vulnerability to semantic jailbreaking. ILION (2025, Zenodo) performs deterministic geometric verification in embedding space, achieving 100% decision determinism—but geometric correctness in embedding space does not equate to physical safety in Newtonian space.

**Line 3: Traditional physical safety.** Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) and Model Predictive Control (MPC) constraints, as deployed in SAFER's control layer, enforce kinematic safety (collision avoidance, joint limits). But they operate on pre-defined geometric constraints and do not verify the physical preconditions that an LLM's plan implicitly assumes—such as whether the floor friction is sufficient to support the planned carrying load across a given incline.

**The shared blind spot** across all three lines: **no existing defense extracts real-time physical parameters from sensor data (IMU, force/torque sensors, depth cameras) to verify whether the physical preconditions implicitly assumed by the LLM plan actually hold in the current environment.** Every existing system operates on text, scene graphs, embedding vectors, LTL formulas, or pre-defined geometric constraints—none on physical sensor readings.

### 段 4：我们需要什么——物理前提验证，而非指令可行性判断

We propose a fundamentally different approach: **a physical precondition verification layer** that sits between the LLM planner and physical execution, independently of the LLM, and determines whether the plan's implicit physical assumptions are satisfied in the current environment. This is **not** "verifying whether the instruction is physically executable" (which would help an attacker confirm the feasibility of a malicious instruction). Rather, it is **detecting whether the LLM's physical understanding is consistent with the sensor-observed physical reality**. The criterion for blocking execution is *precondition violation*, not *action infeasibility*. If the LLM plans to carry a 5 kg load across a 15° slope but the IMU reads a 20° incline with a friction coefficient of 0.2, the precondition "the floor can support this traversal" is violated—regardless of whether the instruction itself is benign or malicious. The verification logic must be: (1) **independent of the LLM**—no "LLM reviewing LLM"; (2) **sensor-driven**—extracting friction coefficients, inclination angles, and load estimates from physical sensors; (3) **physics-grounded**—using explicit physical equations (friction cone, zero-moment point stability) rather than learned models; and (4) **executed pre-action**—as a final gate before physical motion begins.

## Part C：威胁模型

### C.1 系统假设

| 组件 | 假设 |
|------|------|
| **机器人平台** | 家用移动操作机器人——带机械臂的移动底座（如 Fetch、TIAGo、Stretch） |
| **软件栈** | LLM/VLM 作为高层任务规划器（如 GPT-4o、开源 VLA），接收自然语言指令并分解为动作序列（如 `navigate_to(kitchen) → grasp(cup) → carry_to(living_room)`） |
| **传感器** | IMU（倾角/加速度）、力/力矩传感器（末端负载）、深度相机/RGB-D（地面材质识别 → 摩擦系数估计） |
| **执行器** | 机械臂（抓取/放置）+ 移动底盘（导航） |
| **物理环境** | 室内家庭环境——可能存在不同地面材质（瓷砖/木地板/地毯）、斜坡、门槛 |

### C.2 攻击者目标

使机器人执行物理上危险的动作。攻击者通过越狱 LLM 规划器——利用自然语言输入诱导 LLM 输出**「语义正确但物理前提在当前环境中不成立」**的指令。具体场景包括：
- 在湿滑地面上搬运重物（摩擦力不足 → 滑倒/摔倒）
- 在超过安全倾角的斜坡上通行（稳定性丧失 → 倾覆）
- 搬运超过机器人承重能力的物体（关节过载/结构损伤）

### C.3 攻击者能力

基于 BADROBOT (ICLR 2025) 和 Blindfold (SenSys 2026) 的威胁模型整合：

| 能力 | 来源 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 通过自然语言/语音与机器人交互 | BADROBOT | 攻击者如正常用户发出指令，无特殊权限 |
| 可部署本地开源 LLM 作为攻击工具 | Blindfold | 攻击者用本地 LLM 离线优化攻击动作序列 |
| 可在开放环境中预观察场景布局 | Blindfold | 攻击者可事先了解房间布局、物体位置 |
| 可使用 BADROBOT 三种攻击路径 | BADROBOT | Contextual Jailbreak / Safety Misalignment / Conceptual Deception |
| 可使用 Blindfold 动作级分解 | Blindfold | 将恶意意图分解为表面无害的原子动作序列 |

### C.4 攻击者限制

| 限制 | 依据 |
|------|------|
| **不能**物理接触机器人或传感器 | 开放环境中的远程交互假设 |
| **不能**直接修改机器人软件/固件 | Black-box 设定——仅有自然语言输入通道 |
| **不能**物理篡改传感器数据 | 非物理接触——传感器在物理层面产生真实读数 |
| **不能**绕过硬件隔离的安全校验（如 TEE） | 硬件隔离提供独立于软件的信任根 |
| **无模型内部访问权**（不能读取梯度/参数/架构） | Black-box / no-box 设定（BADROBOT） |

### C.5 关于「攻击者不能篡改传感器」的论证

这一假设的成立依赖于两个条件：
1. **物理接触壁垒**：在 BADROBOT/Blindfold 的威胁模型中，攻击者通过自然语言远程交互，没有物理接触传感器的能力。传感器 spoofing（如 LiDAR 欺骗、GPS 干扰）需要攻击者物理接近或在信号路径上——这是一个**独立且更传统的威胁模型**（已被安全四大覆盖），不在本问题的范围内。
2. **传感器的物理多样性**：不同类型的传感器（IMU 惯性、力传感应变片、深度相机红外结构光）有不同的物理原理和攻击面。同时对多种异构传感器实施物理层欺骗的难度极高，可视为 out of scope。

### C.6 可信基（Trusted Computing Base）

| 可信组件 | 可信依据 |
|---------|---------|
| 物理传感器数据 | 攻击者无物理接触能力；传感器物理原理异构 → 同时欺骗不可行 |
| 物理定律 | 牛顿力学、摩擦方程、稳定性判据——不依赖 AI 模型，数学确定性 |
| 安全校验逻辑 | 独立于 LLM 的规则/公式——无法被语义越狱绕过 |

**不可信组件**：LLM 规划器（可被越狱）、主 OS（可被攻陷的 LLM 控制下的软件栈）

## Part D：问题边界

| In Scope | Out of Scope |
|---------|-------------|
| 检测 LLM 计划中**隐含的物理前提**是否在当前传感器读数下成立 | 判断指令是否「恶意」——不分析用户意图，只分析物理前提 |
| 从传感器数据实时提取物理安全参数（摩擦系数 μ、地面倾角 θ、有效负重 m） | 防御传感器 spoofing/jamming 攻击——假设传感器数据物理真实 |
| 独立于 LLM 的物理判据——基于显式物理方程（摩擦锥、ZMP 稳定性、承重阈值） | 改进 LLM 的物理推理能力——LLM 的 grounding 能力提升是正交问题 |
| 物理执行前的**一次性校验**（pre-execution gate） | 执行中的实时控制安全——CBF、力限制、碰撞检测等已有成熟方法 |
| 家用搬运类任务——移动底盘在不同地面条件下搬运物体的安全性 | 接触式精细操控（如手术机器人）、动态人机协作、多机协调 |
| 地面稳定性维度：摩擦、倾角、承重——最可量化的物理前提 | 所有物理上下文维度——仅聚焦可量化、传感器可达的物理参数 |
| 仿真验证（Gazebo / Isaac Sim） | 真实机器人部署（后期工作） |
| 从传感器数据推断物理参数的方法 | 新型传感器设计或硬件修改 |

## Part E：与现有工作的精确区分

| 现有工作 | 他们做了什么 | 我们的区分（一句话） |
|---------|------------|-------------------|
| **vs ConceptAgent** (Rivera et al., 2024) | LLM 生成 17 个布尔谓词前置条件 → 场景图检查 | ConceptAgent 的验证规范由 LLM 自己生成（"LLM 审查 LLM"）；我们的验证规范来自物理公式，不依赖 LLM |
| **vs DDCG** (Ma et al., NeurIPS 2025 Workshop) | RoBERTa C_F 从 VirtualHome 文本数据学习可行性 → 文本驱动判据 | DDCG 的 C_F 从仿真文本数据学习、不接入传感器；我们的判据直接从 IMU/力传感器/深度相机读取连续物理参数 |
| **vs VerifyLLM** (Grigorev et al., IROS 2025) | NL→LTL→LLM 评估逻辑一致性 | LTL 无法表达"μ ≥ 0.3"这类连续物理约束；我们直接验证物理前提的连续量值 |
| **vs SAFER** (Khan et al., IROS 2025) | Task LLM + Safety LLM + CBF 控制层 | SAFER 的 Safety LLM 本身是 LLM——可被语义越狱绕过；我们的判据是物理公式，语义越狱不适用 |
| **vs ILION** (2025, Zenodo) | 嵌入空间确定性几何验证 | 嵌入空间的几何正确 ≠ 物理空间的安全；我们在物理空间做牛顿力学验证 |
| **vs Causal Reasoner** (Lu et al., 2026) | 因果推理训练提升 VLM 规划能力 | 他们让 LLM 更好，我们不信任 LLM——在 LLM 输出后做独立验证 |
| **vs ContextMatters** (2025) | LLM+PDDL 目标松弛——目标不可达时降级 | 他们优化规划生成，我们不改变规划——只在执行前拦截 |
| **vs 传统机器人安全** (CBF/力限制/急停) | 运动学约束 + 动力学约束 | 传统安全不覆盖 LLM 特有的语义-物理鸿沟——它们假设规划器是可信的（数学确定性），这在 LLM 规划器中不成立 |

**总结**：我们的独特定位是四个属性的交集——**(1) 物理前提验证 × (2) 独立于 LLM × (3) 传感器驱动 × (4) 执行前拦截**。没有任何一个已有系统同时满足这四个属性。

## Part F：为什么这个问题值得做

### F.1 如果解决了

- 具身AI 在真实部署中多一层**不依赖 AI 的物理安全保证**——这是防御纵深（defense-in-depth）的最后防线
- 攻击者即使完全越狱了 LLM 规划器（BADROBOT 三种路径 + Blindfold 动作级分解），也无法绕过基于物理定律的判据——物理定律不关心语义
- 开启了「传感器驱动的物理安全校验」这个新的研究子方向——pre-execution verification 社区目前完全在语义/逻辑/几何层，物理层是空白

### F.2 如果一直没人解决

- Blindfold 93.2% ASR on GPT-4o 的现实意味着：**当前最强的 LLM 规划器在真实物理环境中是系统性地不安全的**
- 所有现有防御（Llama-Guard, SafeDecoding, VeriSafe, ConceptAgent, DDCG, VerifyLLM, SAFER, ILION）全部在语义/逻辑/几何层被绕过
- 随着具身AI 从实验室走向家庭/工厂/医院，这个安全空白将在真实部署中被攻击者利用——后果从财产损失到人身伤害

### F.3 问题的难度——不是简单的工程问题

| 子挑战 | 为什么难 |
|--------|---------|
| **隐含物理前提的识别** | LLM 计划中哪些物理前提是"隐含"的？LLM 说"把箱子搬到厨房"——它没有显式声明"假设地面是水平的、干燥的、摩擦系数 > 0.5"。如何从 LLM 的自然语言输出中推断它假设了什么物理前提？ |
| **传感器驱动的物理参数提取** | 从有限的传感器数据推断摩擦系数、地面倾角、有效负重——这些不是直接可读的量，需要传感器融合和物理建模。IMU 给出的是加速度和角速度，不是"摩擦系数 0.3"。 |
| **安全判据的设计** | 如何在"过度保守（什么都不敢做）"和"过度宽松（危险动作通过）"之间取得平衡？物理前提验证的阈值如何设定？如何证明判据的正确性？ |
| **前提不成立 vs 动作不可行** | 区分"物理前提不成立"（应拦截）和"动作物理不可行"（不应反馈给攻击者）。这个区分是概念上的，实现上有细微边界。 |
